# Dr. Stache / Smoothface / MoonStorm art index

Built 2026-10-18 late evening (America/Costa_Rica) from Alexander's Gmail and Drive (read only) and from the recovered demo archives. No executables were run. Duplicates were removed by MD5 hash (identical copies are listed under "also in").

## Counts

- `email/`: 26 images (Gmail attachments plus 1 Drive drawing): Dr. Stache and Smoothface sketches, 11 character sketches, art style tests, Substampede scenes, GUI mock-up, team logos.
- `demo/`: 69 unique image assets from inside LolDemo1 (2010-06-23 and 2010-06-29), LolGame1 (Visionaire) and SubstampedeTest1 (Torque). The builds only contain PNG/JPG (no TGA, DDS or BMP). Torque engine default images under `common/` were skipped.
- `psd_composites/`: 9 full renders of the 9 Photoshop files (psd-tools).
- `psd_layers/`: 107 individual layers rendered as PNG, one subfolder per PSD.
- Original PSDs are not duplicated here. They are inside the .7z files in `recovered/builds/` (on the PC: `recovered_builds_part2_art_archives.zip`), plus `recovered/images/scenery_template.psd`.

Contact sheets: `CONTACT_SHEET_1.jpg` to `CONTACT_SHEET_4.jpg` (everything except layers), `CONTACT_SHEET_PSD_LAYERS_1.jpg` to `_4.jpg` (layers).

Checked and excluded (not Moonstorm art): memes and photos in other 2010-2011 threads (tumblr images, wtf.png, theinternet.jpg, b52_tf2.png, spy_camouflage.jpg, ashley me3.jpg, AWSTREHLEA.jpeg), the photo DSC00387.JPG, desktop.JPG, the website screenshot 01_1920x1080.png, the seal meme, the Torque DEP error screenshot, and chaos_marine003ab.PNG (Aguilar's Warhammer painting exercise, kept in recovered/other). No Gmail inline images with Stache art were found beyond the attachments already listed.
