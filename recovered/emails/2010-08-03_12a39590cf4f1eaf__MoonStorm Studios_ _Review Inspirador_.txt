The DEFCON British developer, Introversion, describes how they hit hard
times, and sold off furniture and fired staff to become a bedroom developer.
At the point they were ready to rewrite their C.V.s and look for jobs when
they came up with a last-ditch rescue plan involving creating Steam
achievements for DEFCON and convincing Valve to run a promotion with it.
Here's the happy ending to the story: "*Right on cue, Valve delivered. The
promo exceeded all of our expectations and when combined with our low burn
rate (no office or staff now) we had gone from being fearful about paying
our mortgages to having a year’s operating capital in the bank. This was
good, but paled in comparison to the fact that we were working together as a
team again. Just like the old days. Just me Chris, TJ and Johnny – laptops,
bedrooms and plans for global domination. The difference this time was we
have a lot more knowledge and experience about what works and what doesn’t.
On the face of it we are less capable than we were six months ago, but
personally I believe that we are ready to rebuild the company bigger and
stronger in a different mould*."

http://forums.introversion.co.uk/defcon/introversion/viewtopic.php?t=2512
