Rupert arrives at Blackspit Island, a known hideout and hub for evil
organizations around the globe.

Some deem its inhabitants as second or even third class evil doers, but
none the wiser, Rupert ventures into it.

STORY OUTLINE

1.  Rupert boards the Substampede, a mysterious and ghostly old
    submarine whose passengers must pedal for the propulsion to work.
    Its only destination is the dreaded Blackspit Island and costs 2
    pennies (also offers a family pack on summer! call now!) on his way
    to Blackspit, thinking about his plans.

2.  Rupert arrives at Blackspit Island.

3.  Goes to a local circus that has been stranded in the island for
    years after a deranged parrot set fire to their cargo ships. They
    don’t have any money to pay for the Substampede to get them off the
    island, and cannot put a show to collect money since all the evil
    inhabitants have stolen the animals to adopt as pets for their evil
    lairs and the employees have gone their own ways:  
    The Mimes defected and formed a band called The Air Guitars, yet no
    one pays attention to them.  
    The Clowns formed a gang, Serious Business, yet people are not
    afraid of them or their heists since they think they’re joking (they
    use props for guns, like water pistols).  
    The Ventriloquist was hired at the Artificial Thundercano Hideout as
    an announcer.  
    The Midgets were hired as body guards at the Artificial Thundercano
    Hideout.

1.  first job: steal candy from “A Baby” (formerly known as
    Facepuncher): A Baby is one of the most feared and respected (if
    such thing exists among evildoers) ex-members of the League of
    Terror. He’s a 2.5 meters, 300 pounds of thundering muscle of a
    beast who mumbles like a baby after he fell on his head from a high
    tower during a fight with his arch enemy El Chulethal. He’s fond of
    his candy collection and would go to great lengths to punish anyone
    who dares to meddle with it. He’s afraid of midgets.

2.  second job: deceive the midgets: Dread requests that Rupert somehow
    get the midgets on strike and decide to return to the circus. He
    must give the ventriloquist a forged note where a false new policy
    states that within a week, all security personnel must meet a
    “minimum height” to keep their position.  
    Explanation: you steal the candy from A Baby, he gets angry, while

    Dread tries to position himself as the "good guy" by returning the
    candy to A Baby in exchange for Lord Goat's "title" as master of the
    **MGWDBTTGPnP**. Ridiculous spin must be introduced to leave Rupert

    people left on the island are evil, they cannot act their wicked
    agenda.

5.  Captain Dread tells Rupert that, in order to “climb the food chain”
    and be eligible as a League of Terror member, he needs a title from
    the Evil Deeds College:

    1.  attends a Maniacal Laughter and Advanced Villainous Speech
        Basics 101 class

    2.  Diabolic Machines of Destruction class

    3.  attends a Kung FOOM! class

    4.  slips into rector’s office, forges evil title (with parts picked
        from the various classes, creates a Rube Goldberg title forging
        machine).

6.  Rupert is taken to the Artificial Thundercano Hideout to be
    introduced to Lord Goat for the recruitment ceremony.

7.  Shit hits the fan! A Baby breaks into the Artificial Thundercano
    Hideout and demands the candy back.
